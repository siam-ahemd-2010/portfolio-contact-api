## 📂 Included Projects

### 📦 Book Scraper

Extracts product information from BooksToScrape and exports structured Excel files.

Collected Data:

- Product Name
- Price
- Rating
- Stock Status
- Product URL

---

### 🛒 Daraz Product Scraper

Automates product collection from Daraz search results.

Collected Data:

- Product Name
- Price
- Rating
- Discount
- Sold Count
- Stock Status
- Product URL

Exports everything to Excel.

---

### 🛍 Amazon Product Scraper

Automates product extraction from Amazon search results.

Collected Data:

- Product Name
- Price
- Rating
- Product URL

Exports structured Excel files.

---

### 📩 Portfolio Contact System

Complete backend-powered contact system.

Features:

- Contact Form API
- Database Storage
- Email Notification
- Automatic Reply Email
